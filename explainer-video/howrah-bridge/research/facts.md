# Fact table: হাওড়া ব্রিজ কীভাবে তৈরি হলো

**How these were checked:** every row below was confirmed through web-search results that quote or summarise the listed pages.
This cloud session could not open the pages directly, because its network policy blocks `wikipedia.org` and most other sites.
So **⚠ rows need a human check** before publishing, and the ✅ rows are worth a quick look at the Wikipedia article.

Confidence:
- ✅ means two or more independent sources agree, or one authoritative source (the company itself, Tata Steel, or Wikipedia with citations) says it.
- ⚠ means sources disagree, or there is only one lower-quality source. The script uses the safe wording shown.
- ✖ means the claim was not verified and is **not used** in the narration.
